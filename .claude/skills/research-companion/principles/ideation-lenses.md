# Structured Brainstorming & Creative Thinking Lenses

Apply these lenses on demand during Phase 1–2 (`SEED` / `DIVERGE`) of `research-companion` to expand candidate directions before evaluation. Always pair with [ideation-patterns.md](ideation-patterns.md) and the [shared topic-selection policy](../../shared-references/topic-selection-policy.md).

## Practical Brainstorming Lenses

1. **Problem-First vs. Solution-First**: Classify the seed; if problem-first verify who suffers and what is genuinely unsolved; if solution-first identify at least two concrete unsolved problems it unlocks.
2. **Abstraction Ladder**: Move **Up** (generalize to a principle/framework), **Down** (instantiate at an extreme constraint or failure case), and **Sideways** (analogize to an adjacent domain).
3. **Tension & Contradiction Hunting**: List 3–5 desiderata (e.g., Performance vs. Efficiency, Safety vs. Capability, Interpretability vs. Accuracy) and ask whether the trade-off is fundamental (characterize Pareto frontier) or an artifact of current methods (reconcile it).
4. **Assumption Auditing & Inversion**: List hidden assumptions of the dominant paradigm and ask what happens if each assumption is relaxed or inverted.
5. **Failure-Mode Mining**: Inspect where strong baselines fail systematically, where evaluation protocols leak or saturate, or where scaling/shift breaks reported claims.

## Cognitive Creativity Engines

1. **Combinatorial Creativity (Bisociation)**: Cross-product core primitives from two distinct domains (A × B) and keep only structural/mechanistic mappings, not surface metaphors.
2. **Problem Reformulation (Representational Change)**: Shift the objective, mathematical formalism, granularity, agent perspective, or timescale (e.g., forward → inverse, per-token → per-span).
3. **Analogical Reasoning (Structure-Mapping)**: Transfer deep causal/relational structure from distant domains rather than surface vocabulary.
4. **Constraint Manipulation**: Artificially add an extreme constraint (10× less compute/data, zero-shot, streaming) or remove a legacy constraint to expose new mechanisms.
5. **Adjacent Possible & Boundary Exploration**: Probe newly enabled capabilities right at the frontier of recent tools, datasets, or theoretical results.
